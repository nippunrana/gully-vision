How Gully-Vision Works

AI-powered technique tracking that bridges the gap between Lucknow maidans and professional cricket pathways.

1

Upload & Input

Upload your batting/bowling/fielding video (MP4/MOV) or paste a YouTube performance link directly.

2

Target the Window

Use the interactive slider to select a key 12-second scouting window covering the setup, release, or impact point.

3

AI Analysis

Gemini 3.5 Flash processes the footage, executing multi-point tracking on joints, head positions, and swing velocities.

4

Get Scouted

View detailed telemetry charts, receive a custom UPCA selector scouting pitch, and claim your spot on the live leaderboard.
